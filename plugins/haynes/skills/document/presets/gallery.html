Kitchen TableForty years of recipe cards

November 26, 2026 – January 3, 2027 · Open whenever someone's cooking

About the exhibitionWall text

Every family keeps two cookbooks: the one on the shelf and the one in the drawer. This exhibition is the second kind.

The cards gathered here were written in pencil, ink and typewriter between 1974 and 2011. Most have been rewritten at least once. Several carry a stranger's handwriting, copied from a neighbor or a potluck. All of them are stained, and the stains are the best record of which recipes were actually made.

They are shown as they were found, grease spots and all, in the order they entered the drawer.
