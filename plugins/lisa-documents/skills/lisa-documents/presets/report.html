PART 1What the technician found

Inspection by the heating company on November 19. The written report is in the kitchen drawer.

High

The heat exchanger is cracked

A crack lets exhaust mix with the air that heats the house. It can leak carbon monoxide, so the furnace should not run until it's fixed.

Either way: space heaters and the carbon monoxide alarm until the work is done.

High

The blower motor is failing

It's running hot and drawing too much power. Replacing it alone costs about $700.

Medium

The ductwork leaks in the basement

Two joints have come apart, so some heat never reaches the upstairs rooms. A small job either way.

Fine

The gas line and thermostat are in good shape

Both can stay as they are, which keeps the installation simple.

PART 2What a new furnace gets us

Safe air

No more worry about a cracked exchanger, and a new carbon monoxide shutoff built in.

Warmer upstairs

A variable-speed blower moves heat more evenly, and the duct fix stops the leaks.

A ten-year warranty

Parts and labor are covered, so a surprise repair doesn't come out of savings.

Lower bills

96% efficient instead of about 78%, which pays back part of the cost every winter.

Good news: the gas company's rebate of $600 is still open through December 31.
