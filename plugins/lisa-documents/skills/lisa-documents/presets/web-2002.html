The sourdough starter lives!NEW!

After three failed tries, the starter on top of the fridge finally doubled overnight. The secret was moving it away from the window. I've named it Gerald.

What finally worked:

  • Equal parts flour and water by weight, fed every morning
  • Unbleached flour only
  • Somewhere warm, about 75°F

First loaf is going in Saturday. Pictures to follow in the photo album.
